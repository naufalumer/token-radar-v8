The model - radar-8: one pooled tilt, an out-of-fold calibration, a backtest, and a plot of the held-out tokens

The version label is the radar's generation count: v2 was radar-3, v3 radar-4, v4 radar-5, v5 radar-6, v6 radar-7. This version, radar-8, changes nothing in the model, only the terminal that presents it: same pooled tilt, same calibration layer, same full formula, same backtest, same board. New here is the presentation: a terminal board with sparklines, chain tickers, a detail chart, a token file that says which fields the snapshot does not carry, and a news tab from snapshot facts only.

implied = calibrate(TVL x fitted multiple x growth x maturity x chain reach), where fitted multiple = category multiple x size tilt

  • Category multiple - median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (3+ peers), else the whole-market median; clamped to 0.02-1.00.
  • Size tilt - one pooled fit, not one per category. Log(market cap) regressed on log(TVL) over all live-token protocols at once (CEX excluded); the tilt is 1 at the market's median live TVL and is applied at half the fitted strength, clamped to 0.50-1.50, because the fit is weak and a full-strength tilt would price the giants far below their own category's peers, an extrapolation, not a measurement.
  • Growth - 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, -50%, +100%) x 0.6.
  • Maturity - 0.70 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 x 0.30; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain reach - 1 + min(chains - 1, 5) x 0.02.
  • Calibration - out-of-fold shrinkage on the full formula. The raw formula's errors are worst in the tails: its median error barely moved against the flat median while its trimmed mean fell hard, which says the middle is fine and the extremes are wrong. So a winsorized log-space line, fitted only on out-of-fold predictions of the live peers, maps log(raw) to log(calibrated). The raw formula's spread is wider than the held-out peers' market-cap/TVL spread, so a monotone shrinkage map compresses its extremes. It improves five of six nested-validation metrics; it does not establish that calibrated values are launch prices. The shift for any one protocol is capped at +-0.75 decades (about 5.6x), and the numbers below are the honest nested measurement, each fold's map fitted without that fold's peers.

Fitting the calibration...

Why one pooled fit: per-category fits were run and rejected, with 5 to 15 live peers per category they lose to the pooled fit on five of six validation checks. Fitting one slope per category sounds more precise; on about 120 tokens it is mostly noise wearing a slope. The within-category slope ties the pooled fit, so it stays out for no gain.

No protocol name is special-cased. The calibration table below is recomputed from whatever snapshot the program is handed, so the radar keeps working on protocols that do not exist yet.

Backtest - the model priced real tokens 30 days ago; here is what happened

Every validation above is cross-sectional: it prices a live token today and compares it with today's market cap. It never asks whether the model predicts what a value becomes. The backtest asks exactly that. On 2026-10-15 the shipped model, every piece fitted on 2026-10-15 data only, priced 113 live-token protocols from their TVL on that day. By 2026-11-14 their realized market caps were known (the sealed snapshot's own figures). Two baselines: the flat category median as of 2026-10-15, and persistence, each token's own market cap on 2026-10-15 carried forward unchanged. A model that cannot beat persistence on levels is a ranker, not a forecaster; the table says which this one is, and the per-category table names where it wins and where it loses. TVL history: DeFiLlama daily TVL per protocol. Market-cap history: CoinGecko daily market cap per token. 6 peers were excluded for missing history (acryptos, fables, frankencoin, kava-lend, pumpbtc, concrete); none were dropped for scoring badly.

Backtest: median error 84.8% for the shipped model, 96.7% for the flat median, 30.3% for persistence. Within twice the realized value: 35.4% shipped, 27.4% flat, 91.2% persistence. Spearman against realized caps: 0.469 shipped, 0.475 flat, 0.985 persistence. The nested backtest, each fold's calibration fitted without that fold's peers, is median error 89.6%, trimmed mean 178.6%, 69 of 113 priced closer than flat, Spearman 0.368, log error 0.664, within 2x 35.4%.
